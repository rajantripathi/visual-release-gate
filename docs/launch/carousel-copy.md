## Slide 1 — AI can observe. It should not decide what ships.

Visual Release Gate

An auditable decision layer for AI-generated creative assets.

## Slide 2 — A model answer is evidence, not authority.

The vision model returns typed observations and exact source IDs.

Deterministic Python validates those citations and applies release invariants.

People retain authority over ambiguity, consent, licensing, ownership, and legal
approval.

## Slide 3 — Four outcomes preserve four meanings.

`SHIP` — no blocking issue is established

`REJECT` — visible evidence establishes a serious violation

`HUMAN REVIEW` — decision-critical pixels support multiple readings

`UNSUPPORTED` — required authority is absent from the evidence

## Slide 4 — The benchmark tests context, not image recognition.

24 fictional cases

8 development · 16 held-out

The same pixels appear under different briefs. Correct decisions must follow the
request, policy, and authority context.

No accuracy claim until independent review and live runs are complete.
