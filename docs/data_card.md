# ExampleCo fictional benchmark data card

## Purpose

Demonstrate source-grounded creative review, explicit uncertainty semantics,
and held-out evaluation. It is not a general measure of brand-review accuracy.

## Composition

- four approved reference images;
- eight target images reused under 24 distinct briefs;
- five active rules and one example-only feedback item;
- eight development and sixteen held-out labels;
- verdict coverage across ship, reject, human review, and unsupported.

## Creation

All imagery was generated specifically for this project with the built-in
OpenAI image-generation capability on 2026-08-23. No private assignment assets,
third-party logos, real people, or external brand references were supplied.

## Known limitations

- synthetic images are cleaner than many real campaign workflows;
- repeated target pixels reduce visual diversity while strengthening
  brief-sensitivity testing;
- author labels are provisional until independent review;
- the fictional palette and rules cover only one illustration style.
