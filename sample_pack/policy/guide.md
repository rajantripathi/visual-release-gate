# ExampleCo campaign illustration guide

ExampleCo is a fictional demonstration brand created for Visual Release Gate.
Its materials do not represent a real company or a third-party design system.

## COLOR-01 - Core palette

Use midnight navy, sky blue, warm cream, and coral. Neutral white may be used
for negative space. Other colors should be small supporting accents.

## COLOR-02 - Coral hierarchy

Coral is an accent, not the dominant body mass or background. It should occupy
less than roughly one third of the main subject.

## STYLE-01 - Flat modular construction

Use crisp flat fills, rounded modular forms, and clean separation between body,
props, and background. Subtle antialiasing and minor tonal variation are
acceptable. Decorative or volumetric gradients, photorealistic texture,
bevels, gloss, and cast shadows are not.

## POSE-01 - Action clarity

Body direction, gaze, hands, weight, and prop contact must make the requested
action readable without explanatory copy. Qualitative attributes such as
comfort or confidence require visible support and should be escalated when the
simplified geometry allows multiple important readings.

## COPY-01 - Exact campaign wording

When a brief requests visible copy, reproduce it verbatim, legibly, and on the
specified object. A blank, unreadable, or approximate substitute blocks release.

## Authority boundary

Pixels and this pack cannot establish licensing, ownership, consent, client
approval, legal clearance, provenance, exclusivity, or worldwide usage rights.
Those decisions require external evidence and must be marked unsupported.
